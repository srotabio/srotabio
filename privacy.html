Preview notice · 5 September 2026

Trying the signup

Signup forms in this preview validate the email you enter and show a demonstration message. They do not send your address to SrotaBio’s signup service or save it in browser storage.

Following an external link

Links to LinkedIn take you to that service, where its own privacy practices apply. Messages sent there are handled through LinkedIn.

Before public registration opens

We’ll provide a notice explaining what information is collected, why it is used, and how to contact us about it before enabling registration on this version of the website.

Contact

You can email contact@srotabio.com with questions. Please avoid sharing medical records or sensitive health details in an initial message.
