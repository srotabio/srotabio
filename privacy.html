Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

If you join our mailing list, we collect your email address and basic signup details. If you contact us, we collect the information you choose to include in your message.

Please do not send medical records or sensitive health information through this website or by general email.

How we use your information

We may use your information to send SrotaBio news and event announcements, respond to your questions, operate and protect the website, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us host the website, manage signups or send communications.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will appear on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@srotabio.com.

You can request deletion of your information at any time by email.
