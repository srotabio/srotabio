Privacy Policy

SrotaBio respects your privacy.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SrotaBio is operated by SrotaFlow Bio Pvt Ltd. This policy covers our website, onboarding, telemedicine, care coordination and related services in India. Services are for adults aged 18 or older. Please read this alongside the consent information provided when you enrol.

Information we collect

Depending on the service, we may collect identity and contact details; health information and care records; consent and appointment records; payment references; support communications; and technical or security logs. We receive information from you and, where authorised and necessary, your care partners. Consultations are recorded only with separate, explicit notice and consent.

How we use it

We use information to provide and coordinate care, communicate with you, process payments, maintain required records, protect our systems and meet legal obligations. Identifiable health information is used for advertising, unrelated marketing, research, model training or product experimentation only after clear information and any separate consent required by law. You can opt out of promotional messages.

Consent and sharing

You may refuse optional information or withdraw consent for future processing by contacting us. This may affect services we can safely provide; legally required retention may continue. We do not sell or rent personal information. Authorised care teams and service providers receive only what they need for their role, subject to confidentiality obligations. Information may also be disclosed for legal obligations, serious threats to safety or a protected corporate transfer.

Storage, retention and security

Providers may process information in India or other locations, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable transfer restrictions. We retain records for service needs, patient safety and applicable legal duties, then securely delete or de-identify them. Protected backups may remain until their normal cycle ends. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and investigate suspected breaches, notifying affected people and authorities where required by law.

Your choices

Subject to applicable law and identity verification, you may request access, correction or deletion, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Retention duties may limit deletion. Email contact@srotabio.com with the subject “Privacy Request” or “Privacy Grievance”. Please avoid unnecessary medical details in your first email. We address grievances within applicable legal time limits.

Website technologies and updates

Necessary cookies or similar technologies may support security and core operation. Optional technologies requiring consent will have a separate choice. We will post policy updates with their effective date and provide additional notice or seek fresh consent where required.
