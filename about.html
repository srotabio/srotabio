Female health is personal!

We believe women deserve healthcare that takes their experiences seriously. SrotaBio begins with that conviction.

From the founder

Abhirath Batra

Why this work
matters to me.

Accompanying my wife through healthcare made the gap between scientific progress and women’s everyday experiences impossible for me to ignore.

I saw how much it matters to have a concern taken seriously, a question answered thoughtfully, and an experience understood in context.

Shilpa’s commitment to female-health research inspired me to approach the problem through the experience of care. That is where SrotaBio began.

We’re starting with PMOS, formerly PCOS, and taking the time to build with the right people. Our ambition is to make empathetic, evidence-led female healthcare more accessible in India.
