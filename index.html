The model we’re building

One view of the person. Clear ownership at every step.

Health rarely arrives in separate boxes. The SrotaBio model is being designed to connect context, clinical judgment and practical support without blurring who is responsible for care.

Context

Start with the whole picture

History, symptoms, priorities, relevant results and everyday realities belong in the same conversation.

Clinical ownership

Keep clinical decisions clinical

Qualified clinicians are intended to own diagnosis, treatment decisions, prescriptions, monitoring and escalation.

Continuity

Support what happens between visits

Practical support is being designed to carry a clinician-led plan into nutrition, movement, sleep and follow-through.

PCOS is now PMOS

A name that reflects a more complete picture.

Polyendocrine metabolic ovarian syndrome (PMOS) is the new name for the condition previously known as PCOS. The change reflects a complex condition with endocrine, metabolic, reproductive and psychological features—not a problem defined only by ovarian cysts.
