We meet you where you are.

We are building clinician-led online care for women with Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS), formerly PCOS—bringing medical care, lifestyle support and follow-through into one connected experience.

PCOS/PMOS care

What women with PMOS tell us

  • I went to 6 doctors and got 6 different answers.
  • “Just lose weight.” That’s the whole plan?
  • Diagnosed with PMOS at 19. Still figuring it out at 32.
  • I want to have a baby this year. Will I be able to conceive?
  • Keto? Low-carb? Fasting? What’s right for my insulin resistance?

So, what are we going to do about it?

We’re building the PMOS care we wish existed.

We’re doing that by:

  1. Bringing all aspects of PMOS care under one roof: medical, nutrition and exercise.
  2. Giving you your own care manager who makes sure you’re heard at every step and the process is smooth throughout.
  3. Helping you build long-term accountability with regular check-ins.

How we deliver care

  1. We get to know you a little better
  2. You meet our expert gynaecologist
  3. Get the tests your doctor recommends
  4. Our partner lab collects samples at your home
  5. Your doctor and health coach create your care plan
  6. We explain your plan to you
  7. If you need medicines, we home deliver them to you
  8. Follow-ups over weeks and months
  9. We track the goals you set with your doctor and coach

Your care manager is there for you every step of the way, listening to your concerns and questions, booking your appointments, and making sure the process is smooth.
